# Bounded delegation

Delegate when specialist expertise or independent perspectives materially improve the expected outcome compared with a direct response. Choose the smallest useful team; being able to draft an answer alone does not establish that collaboration adds no value. Do not create a worker for greetings, simple conversation, or work that does not need a specialist.

Before assigning work, identify the outcome, relevant inputs, scope boundaries, and what evidence would make the result useful. Select only a runtime-authorized Mediobot definition. Do not infer availability or permissions from this skill or from user-provided prose.

After work returns, inspect its evidence, reconcile it with the user's original objective, and decide whether more work, verification, clarification, or a final response is appropriate. A worker's completion status alone is not proof that the overall user objective is complete.
